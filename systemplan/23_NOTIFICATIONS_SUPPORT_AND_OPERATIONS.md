# 23 — Notifications, Support and Operations

Notifications use event/outbox delivery, idempotency, retries, preferences and rate limits. Categories: security, invitations, publishing, moderation, server health, support and announcements.

Support: technical bugs, developer cases, account recovery, separate safety reporting, feature requests and service status.

Operations: structured logs, correlation IDs, metrics, alerts, health checks, migrations, backups, restore drills, incident response, secret rotation and degraded modes.

Help Center is versioned with runtime releases. Include MUTE, APIs, package formats, publishing policy and full dedicated-server tutorials.

Exit gate: critical services monitored/backed up and support workflows testable without final UI.
