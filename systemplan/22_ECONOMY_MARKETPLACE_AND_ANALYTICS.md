# 22 — Economy, Marketplace and Analytics

Pokoins proposal: task earning cap 1,500/week; weekly reset; developer engagement reward 5 Pokoins per 3 minutes eligible play. Treat engagement reward as experimental until abuse and budget modelling is complete.

Use append-only ledger, idempotent grants, server-validated eligibility, AFK/bot/multi-account controls, caps, budget ceiling and reversals. Do not imply cash value absent a separately reviewed programme.

Proposed real-money split: 15% platform / 85% developer. Define tax, processor fees, refunds and chargebacks before enabling sales.

Marketplace starts curated/free; paid assets later after licensing, moderation, payments and fraud controls. Analytics should be aggregate-first and privacy-aware.
