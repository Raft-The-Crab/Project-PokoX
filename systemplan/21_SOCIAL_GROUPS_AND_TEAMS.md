# 21 — Social, Groups and Teams

Keep community groups separate from developer teams. Groups have public/private/invite-only modes, join requests, roles, announcements, member controls, reports and ownership transfer.

Developer teams have owner/admin/programmer/builder/artist/UI designer/tester/publisher roles with fine-grained project permissions.

Social features: friends, follows, blocking, privacy and recent players. Messaging should be limited or deferred until child-safety moderation capacity is ready.

Build entities/permissions/APIs first; test revocation and ownership transfer; UI later.
