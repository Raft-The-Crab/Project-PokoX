## Confirmed achievement expansion rule
Each game starts with 100 achievement slots. Each purchase permanently adds 10 slots to that one game for 150 Pokoins. Purchases are unlimited. Expansions do not transfer between games.

## Planning status
This is a planning baseline. Proposed features are identified as proposals or phased recommendations and are not silently treated as approved commitments. Validate legal requirements, third-party service terms, performance, security and operational capacity before implementation.
