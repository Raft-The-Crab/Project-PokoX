# 30 — Community Safety and Trust

## Safety surfaces

Apply reporting, blocking and rate limits consistently across profiles, posts, comments, forums, groups, chat, direct messages, game pages, reviews, events, creator listings and volunteer applications.

## Moderation layers

- Automated detection may prioritize or flag content but must not be the final authority for formal game approval.
- Community moderators handle assigned public spaces.
- Game reviewers use the isolated Review Runtime and the established human approval workflow.
- A restricted specialist team handles sensitive safety cases.
- Platform administrators manage account-level and platform-wide enforcement under documented policy.

## Community controls

- Report content, user, group, game or event from the relevant surface.
- Block and mute users with clear explanations of effects.
- Restrict invitations, mentions, messages and profile visibility.
- Rate-limit new accounts and suspicious activity.
- Provide appeal/escalation paths appropriate to the action.
- Protect reporters from retaliation and avoid exposing report identities unnecessarily.

## Volunteer safeguards

- Least-privilege role access and supervised probation.
- Training on policy, privacy, evidence handling and escalation.
- Conflict-of-interest disclosure and reassignment.
- Audit logs for report access and moderation actions.
- Immediate revocation on role exit or suspected compromise.
- No shared moderator accounts or reusable static login keys.
- No production credentials distributed to volunteers.

## Direct messages and youth safety

Do not launch unrestricted direct messages without a mature safety and privacy design. Start with conservative defaults, age-appropriate controls, block/report, rate limits, link restrictions, abuse detection and documented retention. Do not expose exact age or sensitive account attributes publicly. Design with applicable child privacy and safety obligations reviewed by qualified counsel.

## Reviews and reputation

Game reviews should be rate-limited and tied to meaningful play or other defensible eligibility. Prevent review brigading, paid positive reviews, retaliation and creator manipulation. Provide a report function and moderation process. Do not let review scores override formal safety approval.
