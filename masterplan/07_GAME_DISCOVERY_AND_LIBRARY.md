# 07 — Game Discovery and Library

## Discovery surfaces
- Featured games selected by staff.
- New and recently updated.
- Genre and tag browsing.
- Search by title, creator or tag.
- Official Poko games.
- Friends' public activity, if enabled.
- Group recommendations.
- Curated collections.
- Accessible/offline-compatible games.

## Game detail page
Display:
- Game title, creator and verified team.
- Description and media.
- Content labels.
- Supported platforms.
- Supported modes: offline, player-hosted, dedicated.
- Controls and accessibility notes.
- Last update date and version.
- Favorite and play actions.
- Report button.
- Known limitations where useful.

## Search
- Exact title matching and typo tolerance.
- Search filters for genre, platform, content labels and play mode.
- Safe-search filtering.
- Do not rank solely by raw playtime or reward farming.
- Prevent keyword stuffing and misleading metadata.
- Provide a clear empty-results state.

## Library
- Installed and downloaded games.
- Recent games.
- Favorites.
- Update queue.
- Offline availability indicator.
- Per-game storage size.
- Uninstall and clear cached data.
- Repair/verify package action.

## Recommendations
Start with transparent, simple ranking:
- Editorial picks.
- Recently updated.
- Genre interest.
- Quality signals and player feedback.
- Diversity so new creators can be discovered.
- Safety and moderation status.

Do not allow paid placement to masquerade as organic recommendations. If sponsored placement is ever introduced, label it clearly.

## Reviews and ratings
If player reviews are enabled:
- Limit spam and review bombing.
- Allow reporting and moderation.
- Separate star ratings from written reviews.
- Avoid incentivizing positive reviews.
- Consider disabling written reviews for young users or at launch.
