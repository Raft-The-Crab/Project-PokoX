# 28 — Community Contribution and Help PokoX

## Purpose

Give players, creators, volunteers, sponsors and supporters safe, transparent ways to help PokoX grow. Community support must never purchase privileged moderation decisions, ownership, user data access or preferential platform treatment.

## Help PokoX hub

Create a dedicated destination in the website and community hub with these sections:

- **Infrastructure goals:** clearly scoped server, storage, bandwidth, monitoring and backup goals.
- **Volunteer roles:** community helper, moderator applicant, QA tester, documentation writer, translator, event host, creator mentor, asset contributor and approved infrastructure volunteer.
- **Community contributions:** tutorials, bug reports, sample projects, localization and approved open-source contributions.
- **Progress updates:** goals, milestones, completed work, delays and post-mortems.
- **Transparency:** target, amount received, fees, amount spent, recurring operating cost, remaining balance and evidence appropriate to publish.
- **FAQ and contact:** eligibility, privacy, refunds where applicable, recognition and how contributions are used.

## Infrastructure support programme

Support can be financial, in-kind or technical:

1. Voluntary financial contributions to a named, time-bounded goal.
2. Server or hosting sponsorship.
3. Cloud credits or legitimate nonprofit/startup credits.
4. Hardware donations, subject to compatibility, ownership and maintenance review.
5. Approved technical volunteer work.

Each goal must state what it funds, why it is needed, target amount, expected one-time and recurring costs, target date, risks, who controls the account, and what happens if the target is missed or exceeded. Publish progress and an outcome report. Do not describe a target as a purchase already made. Do not promise tax deductibility, refunds, financial returns or legal treatment without qualified review. Use a payment provider and legal structure appropriate to the operator's jurisdiction; obtain legal/accounting advice before accepting public funds. Keep contribution accounting separate from Pokoins and the creator economy.

A contribution must not grant voting power over safety decisions, moderation privileges, access to private data, server credentials, special review treatment or guaranteed feature implementation.

## Volunteer lifecycle

1. Role page explains tasks, minimum eligibility, expected time, conduct rules and access level.
2. Application collects only necessary information and includes a privacy notice.
3. Staff review eligibility, skills, availability and conflicts of interest.
4. Selected applicants complete role-specific training and policy acknowledgement.
5. Supervised probation with limited permissions.
6. Role assignment, access grant and audit trail.
7. Regular access review, feedback and retraining.
8. Resignation or removal triggers immediate access revocation and credential rotation where relevant.

Sensitive safety reports, child-safety escalations, private messages and account-security cases are restricted to specifically trained and authorized staff. General volunteers must not see them. Consider adult-only eligibility for sensitive moderation access, subject to applicable law and a documented safeguarding policy. Never use volunteers as a substitute for legally required or professionally qualified safety response.

## Role boundaries

| Role | Allowed work | Not allowed by default |
|---|---|---|
| Community helper | Answer public FAQs, welcome users, direct reports to official channels | View private reports or punish users |
| Forum moderator | Handle public posts, comments and group rules | Access account credentials or private safety cases |
| Game reviewer | Review assigned game submissions in isolated Review Runtime | Access production systems or unrelated player data |
| QA tester | Test designated builds and file reproducible bugs | Publish releases or access production secrets |
| Translator | Translate approved text and report context issues | Change policy meaning or publish unreviewed legal text |
| Documentation contributor | Draft guides and examples | Publish security-sensitive instructions without review |
| Infrastructure volunteer | Work in approved test/staging scope | Hold sole control of production credentials or backups |

## Recognition

Use contribution badges and profile acknowledgements for verified work. Recognition should describe contribution, not imply employment, official authority or endorsement. Avoid leaderboards based on reports closed, bans issued, or raw volunteer hours. Quality, accuracy, safety, reliability and collaboration matter more.

Suggested recognitions: First Contribution, Helpful Member, Documentation Contributor, Bug Finder, Translator, Event Organizer, Creator Mentor, Asset Contributor and Infrastructure Supporter.

## Roadmap and feedback portal

A suggestion has a public status: Submitted, Needs Information, Under Discussion, Planned, In Development, Testing, Released, On Hold or Declined. Users can vote, comment and subscribe to updates. Votes inform prioritization but do not automatically determine roadmap, moderation policy, security decisions or legal compliance. Give a short reason for major declines or deferrals. Rate-limit votes and detect coordinated manipulation.

## Governance and audit

- Publish community rules, volunteer code of conduct and conflict-of-interest policy.
- Require disclosure when a volunteer reviews their own game, a friend's game or a competing creator's work; reassign conflicted cases.
- Log permission grants, sensitive access and moderation actions.
- Provide an appeal or staff escalation route for volunteer conduct complaints.
- Remove access promptly when a role ends.
- Review volunteer programme metrics without exposing private user information.
