# 02 — Open Decisions and Assumptions

Use this list as a decision backlog. Do not turn a recommendation into a confirmed requirement without owner approval.

## Decisions needed before public beta
1. **Review attempt scope:** four attempts per game lifetime or per release submission? Every update is reviewed, so the scope affects long-term publishing.
2. **Appeal scope:** one appeal per rejected submission, per game, or per enforcement case?
3. **Content labels:** final label vocabulary, definitions and player-facing wording.
4. **Age requirements:** minimum account age, age assurance approach, parental controls and regional legal obligations.
5. **Messaging:** launch with friends-only messaging, group chat, game chat, or no direct messages initially?
6. **Groups:** maximum members, public/private/discoverable modes, group ownership transfer and moderation responsibilities.
7. **Creator teams:** whether group membership and game-team membership are separate systems.
8. **Marketplace:** whether paid assets are in scope at launch or only free assets.
9. **Pokoins:** exact earning eligibility, daily/weekly caps, spending sinks, expiry and anti-farming rules.
10. **Revenue:** payment provider, taxes, refunds, chargebacks, developer identity checks and payout minimums if real-money monetization is introduced.
11. **Player-hosted servers:** NAT traversal, relay policy, host migration and availability expectations.
12. **Offline games:** whether account-free downloads are allowed and how offline updates/revocation work.
13. **Data retention:** chat, reports, logs, test recordings and moderation evidence retention periods.
14. **Regions:** launch countries and applicable privacy, consumer, child-safety and reporting requirements.
15. **Analytics:** exact events collected, opt-outs, retention and whether any analytics are shared with developers.
16. **Achievement cap:** confirm proposed 100 total achievements per game, maximum 20 hidden and 10 featured.
17. **Achievement rewards:** confirm whether achievements award only badges/titles/points at launch, with no automatic Pokoins.
18. **Platform achievements:** decide launch set, points display and whether seasonal achievements can expire.
19. **Help PokoX funding:** choose operator/legal entity, payment provider, accounting process, refund policy and public reporting standard before accepting money.
20. **Volunteer eligibility:** define age requirements, identity checks if any, training, probation, conduct rules and access boundaries.
21. **Community launch scope:** decide which of forums, Q&A, DMs, events, roadmap voting and creator collaboration ship at launch versus later.
22. **Design tokens:** approve final brand palette, typography, radius scale, animation timing and accessibility targets.

## Recommended defaults
- Launch with groups and developer teams as separate concepts.
- Launch with friends, follows, blocking and privacy controls before unrestricted direct messaging.
- Launch the marketplace with free assets first.
- Keep Pokoins non-transferable and non-cash-equivalent initially.
- Treat the 5 Pokoins / 3 minutes developer reward as experimental until abuse modelling and a budget cap exist.
- Require a formal review for every update, but preserve the last approved version while the update is pending.
- Use content labels as disclosures, while applying a separate prohibited-content policy.
- Use human approval; automation only assists.
- Keep sensitive moderation evidence in restricted storage with access logging.
- Start with a small number of supported server modes and tested configurations.
- Keep community funding separate from Pokoins and developer rewards; never grant moderation privileges in exchange for contributions.
- Treat the 100-achievement-per-game cap as a proposed launch default, not a confirmed user decision.
- Build the modern colourful 2008-web-inspired visual identity on top of accessible, responsive design-system foundations.

## Decision record format
For each decision record:
- ID
- Date
- Question
- Options considered
- Decision
- Reason
- Consequences
- Owner
- Review date
